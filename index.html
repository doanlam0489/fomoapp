Overview

What is Fomo? Understanding Fomo Family & Fomo Trading

Before you chase the referral discount, it helps to know what Fomo Trading actually is and how it differs from a traditional exchange.

What the Fomo app does

Fomo (often called Fomo Family) is a social-first crypto trading app launched in May 2025. You scroll a live feed of other people's buys and sells, follow traders, and trade memecoins and altcoins directly — no seed phrase, no separate wallet to manage.

How Fomo Trading works

The app connects straight to public chains like Solana, Base and BNB Chain. Fund with Apple Pay, a card, or a crypto transfer, then buy or sell a token in a couple of taps instead of juggling multiple wallet-connect tabs.

What makes it a "Family"

The social layer is the differentiator: follow KOLs, watch the community's trades in real time, and build your own referral network under your own username.

Team & funding: according to public reporting, Fomo was founded by Paul Erlanger and Se Yong Park, former colleagues at dYdX. The company has raised funding including a Series A led by Benchmark, with backing from investors connected to Solana and Polygon, and a later Series B led by Index Ventures and Union Square Ventures.
Fomo Crypto & risk

Is Fomo crypto trading safe?

Trading memecoins on any platform — Fomo included — carries very high volatility risk. Nothing here is investment advice.

What to know before you trade

Memecoins can swing dozens of percentage points in minutes. Following other traders is useful context, not a guarantee of profit. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and do your own research (DYOR) before you buy.

Always verify with the source

Check fees, terms and current features directly in the Fomo app or on fomo.family, since figures on independent guides like this one can change over time.

How to download Fomo and enter referral code Welcomes

The referral code is only credited if you enter it while creating your account. Follow this order so you don't lose the discount.

  3. Sign up with email or Apple ID

    No crypto wallet and no 12-24 word seed phrase required. The whole process from download to your first look at the feed usually takes under a minute.

  4. Check the "Referral Code" field before continuing

    Scroll the tokens the community is trading, follow a couple of traders or KOLs with a solid track record, then place your first buy or sell.

FAQ

Fast answers on the Fomo app, Fomo Family & the referral code

What is Fomo?

Fomo (Fomo Family) is a social-first crypto trading app launched in 2025, letting you buy and sell memecoins and altcoins across chains like Solana, Base and BNB Chain without managing a separate wallet or seed phrase.

What is the Fomo Family referral code and what does it unlock?

It's the code you enter at signup so your new account is credited to whoever shared it, typically unlocking around a 10% discount on trading fees. Confirm the exact percentage inside the app.

Can I add a referral code after I've already created a Fomo account?

No. The code only applies during new account creation. Fomo hasn't published a way to attach a code to an existing account.

What are Fomo's trading fees?

Around 0.45% per spot trade, gas included, per the platform's published figures. Fees can change, so always check the confirmation screen.

Is Fomo Trading a centralized exchange (CEX)?

Not quite. Fomo is a social trading layer connecting directly to public chains, which differs from the centralized order-book model of a traditional CEX.
